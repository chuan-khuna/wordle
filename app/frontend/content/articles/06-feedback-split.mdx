A Guess sorts the <Term>Candidate Set</Term> into groups, one per possible <Term>Feedback</Term>. That sorting is the <Term>Feedback Split</Term>.

A good Guess makes many small groups; a bad one leaves a few big ones. Select a bar to see which words share that Feedback, and score the whole split with two numbers, both in the chart's title: the number of Candidates the Guess is expected to leave, and its <Term>Expected Information</Term> in bits.
