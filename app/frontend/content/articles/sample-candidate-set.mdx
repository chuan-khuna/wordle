This is placeholder content, and it builds on [Feedback](/articles/sample-feedback). Each <Term>Feedback</Term> rules out <Term of="Candidate">candidates</Term>, and what is left is the <Term>Candidate Set</Term>.

If the set holds $N$ words and a Guess splits it evenly into $k$ groups, the next set has about $N/k$ words. The <Term>Expected Information</Term> of that Guess is

$$
-\sum_{i} p_i \log_2 p_i
$$

bits, where $p_i$ is the share of the set that falls in group $i$.
